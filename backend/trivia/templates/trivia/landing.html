How it works

  1. Export your data. Google Takeout, select YouTube, export. Google emails you a zip after a few minutes.
  2. Upload watch-history.json. The file from the YouTube and YouTube Music folder inside the zip. Nothing is sent to Google, and your history is only ever shown to you.
  3. Get your interests. Your top channels, weighted by how much of your history each one accounts for.
  4. Take the quiz. Questions written for your topics, and a score at the end that tells you which channels you have been half watching.

The honest parts

  • Your history stays yours. Uploaded rows belong to your account only, and no page exposes them to anyone else.
  • Quiz generation is capped. A few quizzes a day, so one person cannot spend the site's budget.
  • It works without JavaScript. HTMX makes it feel quick, but every question can be answered by a plain form post.
